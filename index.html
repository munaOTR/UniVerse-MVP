01 · Overview

The campus experience is fragmented.

Students currently move between separate platforms for conversations, academic resources, communities and campus interactions. UniVerse ICOS is designed to bring these experiences into one connected environment.

ICOS — Integrated Campus Operating System

ICOS is the broader architecture behind UniVerse ICOS: a connected digital environment designed around the different ecosystems of campus life — social, academic, communication and collaboration.

UniVerse ICOS v1.0

UniVerse ICOS v1.0 is the first practical layer of that vision — a focused MVP built around four core systems, with the wider ICOS ecosystem expanding from here.
